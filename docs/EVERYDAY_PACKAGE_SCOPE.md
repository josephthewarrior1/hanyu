# Everyday situations package - bounded implementation plan

This follows the separately delivered 0.4.1 correction. Preserve the corrected follow-up START-time rule, signing certificate, offline/no-login use and all existing learner data. No advanced/expert placeholder levels, official alignment or universal fluency promise.

Six units: routines/people; arranging date/time/place; travel/directions; food/preferences; shopping/comparison/quantities; describing a recent event/problem and asking for help. Each has two instructional lessons and one cumulative scenario (18 total). Target 84 varied guided tasks, 12 original four-to-six-turn dialogue variants, six short readings, 12 optional typed-message prompts and optional private recording prompts. Recycle the existing 82 entries and add 103 explicitly introduced words/chunks; current unique count has been checked. A chunk is counted as an entry, not misrepresented as a unique Mandarin word.

Language is original generated material with bilingual ID/EN notes/prompts/feedback. Dictionary pinyin and connected changes are kept distinct. Notes introduce a pattern before its controlled tasks. Longer reading/writing and open conversation beyond these situations remain outside this package. Qualified human linguistic/acoustic review is absent and remains prominently stated.

Four held-out forms contain 20 items each: ten listening, five reading and five controlled production. Their contexts/utterances are withheld from instructional examples. Reading displays its passage; listening hides the script unless deliberately revealed as help. Each dimension must reach at least 80% on its first attempts without assistance; an aggregate cannot mask weak listening. Wrong-first retries and hint/reveal remain in history.

The normal app first exposes an entire form when it is opened. Persist that conservative exposure timestamp plus item first-attempt timestamps. Abandoned/previously opened forms remain practice, never fresh evidence. A distinct unseen form must be opened at least 24 hours after an earlier qualifying pass, on a later profile-calendar day. A later-week check uses a third distinct unseen form at least seven days after the initial qualifying pass and after the qualifying next-day form. These are bounded app rules, not certifications or proof of speaking competence. Finite forms can run out; show that limit honestly.

Private typed messages and recordings count attempted practice only. Do not grade open writing, assess pronunciation or infer competence from a self-impression. No live AI, paid API, login, cloud sync or hosting is added. Generated offline speech keeps the existing provenance and explicit review limitation.

Implementation: isolated bridge curriculum/UI and native SQLite tables, additive database migration 2 to 3, version code 5 / 0.5.0-android using the existing signer. Existing starter/legacy run IDs, scores, help usage, reviews and points remain unchanged. Include new vocabulary in real SRS and provide resumable lesson/scenario runs, idempotent completions, per-skill checks, drafts/history/export and retrieval due dates. Save clearly labelled source/APK checkpoints before long validation.

Verification: content counts/uniqueness and audio coverage; real Java/native SQLite grading, per-dimension thresholds, exposure/assistance/idempotency, early-opened delayed-Finish regression, positive next-day/week clock tests, private practice not mastery, additive upgrade retaining every old row, offline UI lesson/scenario/message/check and restart flow, signature/asset/archive checks and honest evidence/backlog. No real learner proficiency experiment or acoustic validation will be claimed.
