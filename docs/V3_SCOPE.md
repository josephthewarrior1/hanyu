# v3 implementation scope and curriculum audit

Existing Android v2:10 units,30 lessons,60 words,210 exercises and120 generated clips. All30 lessons use the same seven text-oriented templates and two focus words. Pinyin distractors are unrelated words, not tone contrasts; dialogue is a single cloze. Hints incorrectly refer to word A even for word B questions. Basic identity/question/classifier words appear before explicit instruction. Review repetition alone is not evidence of listening or speaking mastery.

Chosen next release:4 foundation modules +6 practical goals, with12 two-turn dialogue variants,6 scored activities per module (60 new guided activities), and3 fresh mixed forms of10 items (30 assessment activities).22 functional words/phrases are introduced explicitly alongside existing vocabulary. Compact initials/finals charts support four foundation modules; they do not promise every Mandarin syllable combination or advanced phonology.

Sequence: syllables/tone marks → four tones/neutral tone → aspiration, ü/u and nasal contrasts → connected speech (third tone, 一/不) → introduce self → request repetition → order a drink → ask/understand price → ask/understand location → simple time plan. 是, 吗, 什么, 不, 没有, classifiers and simple time word order are taught before applications that use them.

Product mastery is scoped to these practical goals, never fluency or HSK/CEFR certification. It requires >=80% first-attempt unhinted on10 mixed listening/reading/application items and a fresh different form >=80% at least24h later on a later profile-calendar day. Reusing a seen form is practice, not fresh evidence. Failed/used forms remain repeatable; finite new forms and their limitations are shown honestly. Recognition, listening and attempted recording are separate. V2 completions/repetition counts do not backfill new mastery.

Tone/minimal-pair TTS examples are explicitly provisional natural generated speech, excluded from mastery until acoustic linguistic review. No intentionally incorrect synthetic pronunciation is created. All audio questions hide Hanzi/pinyin/answer-leaking labels; audio failure does not become a wrong attempt. Dictionary spelling and connected pronunciation are distinguished. Recording/replay is optional self-check, not assessed pronunciation. Original generated curriculum and audio have not been reviewed by a human linguist.

No account, live AI conversation, paid credential, hosting or backend change. Existing signature, local progress and30 legacy lessons are retained. Advanced material is marked unavailable with a concrete coverage explanation. Source/candidate checkpoints go to Library before final validation to survive disconnects.

Reviewer guidance received through parent: short foundation→dialogue→delayed retrieval path; do not inflate templates; distinguish 是/adjectives, 没有/不, 二/两, WH/吗 questions, tone marks, polyphones and natural neutral tones. Reference: [MIT tone teaching](https://web.mit.edu/jinzhang/www/pinyin/tones/) and [MIT Chinese I](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21g-101-chinese-i-regular-fall-2014/). These inform original explanations, not a copied licensed course or an endorsement.
